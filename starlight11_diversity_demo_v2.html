Starlight 11 · Media literacy

Practice: claims, evidence and careful language

Один языковой фокус, но разные действия: выбрать по сцене, собрать аргумент, поймать ловушку, сопоставить функцию, ответить в диалоге и написать короткий собственный вариант.

1 · выбери по сцене

What should they check first?

Three students are discussing a viral post before using it in a school project.

Best answer: Who published it and whether it is advertising.
2 · собери аргумент

Put the argument in the strongest order

  1. Constant notifications can interrupt deep work.
  2. Students still need digital tools for research and communication.
  3. However, this does not mean that phones should be banned completely.
  4. A better solution is to set clear phone-free periods during lessons.
Здесь ученик выстраивает логику: problem → balance → concession → solution.
3 · поймай ловушку

What is wrong with the claim?

“The article proves that all online reviews are fake.”

Best answer: Too strong: “all” is not supported.
4 · соедини функцию и фразу

Match the phrase to its job

It could be argued that...
introduces another view
This suggests that...
draws a conclusion
For instance,...
adds an example
Это тренирует дискурс, а не повторяет соседнюю форму ответа.
5 · ответь в диалоге

Choose Mia’s reply

Alex: This source says energy drinks improve memory. We can use it as proof.
Mia: ...
Best answer: Maybe, but we should check who funded the study before calling it proof.
